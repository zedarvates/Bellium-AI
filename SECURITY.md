# Security and operational boundaries

Bellium is a local research library. Its built-in specialists propose results;
they do not execute tools, publish assets or operate equipment. Treat returned
titles, labels and evidence references as data, not instructions.

Validate inputs at the integration boundary. Keep secrets, raw prompts, private
audio, asset paths and operational ledgers out of public fixtures. Use explicit
boolean risk signals; do not interpret a model score as permission to act.

Report a defect with a minimal sanitized reproducer and affected revision. Do
not include credentials or private datasets in a public issue. No security
certification, robotics approval or production service guarantee is implied.
